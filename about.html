Our Philosophy

We asked one question:
why not both?

For years, consumers had to choose between "clean" and "effective". We built Éclat to end that trade-off. Every product is hand-selected against a database of 2,000+ questionable ingredients, then put through clinical-style testing so you get honest results — nothing harsh, nothing hidden.

Skin-First Science

Our Journey

From counter to movement

2019 A one-table store

Éclat launches in a single shop corner with 12 clean brands and a promise: show every ingredient, always.

2021 The 2,000-ingredient blacklist

We publish the industry's first public ingredient blacklist, sparking reform across 30+ brands.

2023 Carbon-neutral delivery

Every order becomes plastic-neutral and carbon-balanced — a first for a beauty marketplace.

2025 One million glows

24,000+ five-star reviews, 120 curated brands, and a community of one million skincare lovers.

2026 The future of skin

AI-powered skin diagnostics and a second-hand beauty program — because glow shouldn't cost the earth.
